## 7. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. **How do I book a carpool ride?**
   * *Search for your departure and arrival cities, pick a date, choose a driver that matches your schedule, and tap "Book". You can pay in cash directly to the driver or via supported online methods.*
2. **How does ride cancellation work?**
   * *If a driver cancels, you receive an immediate alert and can book an alternative ride. If you cancel at least 24 hours in advance, there are no penalties.*
3. **What can I bring in terms of luggage?**
   * *Drivers specify luggage capacity (Small bag, Medium suitcase, or Large suitcase) in their ride details.*
4. **Is instant booking safe?**
   * *Yes! Instant booking confirms your seat immediately without waiting for the driver's manual approval, backed by verified member profiles.*
